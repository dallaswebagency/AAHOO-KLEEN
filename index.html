07 / FAQs

Questions, answered

Still not sure about something? Give us a call and we'll walk you through it.

Read More Questions
Q.01How long does a hood system cleaning take?+
A standard hood system cleaning takes 3 to 5 hours.
Q.02How do I know that the entire system was serviced?+
Our technicians take photos of the cleaned areas and email them to you, noting any deficiencies.
Q.03How often does a hood system need to be cleaned?+
Most restaurants and commercial kitchens need quarterly service. Systems using solid fuel need monthly service. Low-grease locations such as schools, churches or seasonal sites may only need annual service.
Q.04Do I have to be on site for the entire cleaning?+
No. You can provide a key, or tell the technician how to lock up when the job is done.
Q.05Are filters included in the price?+
Filters are not included, but we can clean them on request for an extra fee.
Q.06What type of chemical do you use?+
We use a diluted sodium hydroxide solution.
